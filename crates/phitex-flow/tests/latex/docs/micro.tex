\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{microtype}
\begin{document}
\section{Protrusion}

A quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, and then (again) it jumps over
the lazy dog once more. ``Quick,'' said the fox --- and it jumped. The end
of the story: dogs are lazy, foxes are quick; and so on, and so forth,
until the end of the paragraph is reached at last, which it is, here.

``Hyphens-and-dashes'' at the end of lines, commas, periods, and quotation
marks hang into the margin; so do the left parentheses (like this one) and
the opening quotes. Lines are broken knowing it: the paragraph's breaks
come out differently from those without protrusion, as they should, since
each line is a little wider than it looks.

\begin{minipage}{0.5\textwidth}
A narrow minipage, whose lines protrude too: ``margins,'' hyphens-, and
dots. More text here, to have more lines; and still more, until it ends.
\end{minipage}

\microtypesetup{protrusion=false}
Protrusion off again: ``margins,'' hyphens-, and dots, as plain \TeX\ sets
them, with the same words as above to compare their lines, line by line.

\microtypesetup{protrusion=true}
\noindent\textit{Italic text, also protruding: ``quotes,'' dots... and more
words to fill some lines; italic fonts have their own codes, as they should.}
\end{document}
