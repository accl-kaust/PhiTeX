\def\fewwords{The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, and the lazy dog watches the quick
brown fox jump again; typesetting systems break such paragraphs into lines by minimising
badness, and a small stretch or shrink of each font lets the lines come out more even than
the interword glue alone allows. Hyphenation, protrusion of punctuation into the margin and
expansion of the glyphs all take part in the choice of the breakpoints.}
