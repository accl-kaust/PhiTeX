% Font expansion (\pdfadjustspacing, \pdffontexpand) and character protrusion as the micro-typography
% package sets them: pdfLaTeX breaks the paragraph with expanded fonts and writes the expansion as a
% horizontal scale (Tm); PhiTeX breaks it with the fonts as they are (other line breaks, no Tm).
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{microtype}
\begin{document}
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, and the lazy dog watches the quick brown fox
jump again; typesetting systems break such paragraphs into lines by minimising badness,
and a small stretch or shrink of each font lets the lines come out more even than the
interword glue alone allows. Hyphenation, protrusion of punctuation into the margin and
expansion of the glyphs all take part in the choice of the breakpoints.
\end{document}
