\centerline{\bf A SHORT STORY}
\vskip 6pt
\centerline{\sl by A. U. Thor}
\vskip .5cm
\mark{Galaxy}Once upon a time, in a distant
  galaxy called \"O\"o\c c,
there lived a computer
named R.~J. Drofnats.\footnote*{A computer of the old school, with
a front panel of blinking lights.}

Mr.~Drofnats---or ``R. J.,'' as
he preferred to be called---was happiest when he was at work
typesetting beautiful documents.\footnote\dag{Mostly cookbooks.}

\medskip
\noindent{\bf EXERCISE 1.1}\quad\mark{Exercise}Explain why R.~J. was happiest
at work, and what ``beautiful'' means for a document set by a computer. Give
two reasons, and a third if you can find one.

\smallskip
\item{a)} Typesetting is a craft, and craftsmen like their work.
\item{b)} The documents were beautiful, which pleased him.
