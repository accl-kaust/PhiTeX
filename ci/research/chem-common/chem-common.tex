\section{Reactions}
Fischer esterification:
\begin{center}
\ce{CH3COOH + C2H5OH <=>[\ce{H2SO4}] CH3COOC2H5 + H2O}
\end{center}
Combustion of methane, \ce{CH4 + 2 O2 -> CO2 + 2 H2O}, releases
\SI{-890.3}{\kilo\joule\per\mole}; the sulfate ion is \ce{SO4^2-}, heavy
water \ce{D2O}, and the isotope \ce{^{14}_{6}C} decays by
\ce{^{14}_{6}C -> ^{14}_{7}N + e- + \bar{\nu}_e}.
